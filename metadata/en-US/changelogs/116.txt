First public MAZIKA release.

* Android Auto: browse Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists, Genres and Folders, search by voice, and see cover art in the queue. Pick which categories appear and in what order.
* Custom covers for playlists and songs.
* Recently played on For You - the albums, artists and playlists you played from.
* Rewritten drag-to-reorder in playlists, the queue, home tabs and car categories.
* Favourite a song straight from its row.
* Six one-tap theme presets; the launcher icon follows your theme.
